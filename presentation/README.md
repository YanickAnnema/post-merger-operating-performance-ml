# Thesis defense presentation

The 25-minute colloquium deck for the thesis defense (University of Twente, 10 July 2026), as a single self-contained HTML file. This is the public version: the speaker script, presenter view, and rehearsal material are not included, and all numbers shown are aggregate results already disclosed in the published thesis.

## Viewing

Open `index.html` in any browser. No server or build step needed (an internet connection loads the web fonts and the title background image).

Controls:

- `→` / `Space` — next slide, `←` — back
- `f` — fullscreen
- `a` — jump to the appendix (4 extra slides behind the close)
- `Home` / `End` — first / last slide, `#n` in the URL deep-links to slide n

## Content notes

- 28 stage slides plus a 4-slide appendix with the material not shown on stage: how TreeSHAP attribution is computed, why the transfer to SMEs is not copy-paste, what the thesis does not claim, and its three contributions. (The defense version carried a longer Q&A appendix; the slides duplicating the on-stage extension panels were dropped for this public copy.)
- All charts are drawn natively in HTML/CSS/SVG from aggregate thesis results; no licensed row-level data is embedded.
- The illustrative photos in `figs/` are AI-generated images and royalty-free stock used as visual metaphors (the dating-profile analogy, the Christmas-dinner integration story, office cultures). The title background is hotlinked from Unsplash.
